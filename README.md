# The-Mystery-Of-Colors-3D
Try to match colors, win rewards, and pass levels

# Game Explanation
We have a total of 40 tasks, and we must complete them all by combining colors.

Initially, they spawn from a total of 4 locations, but when chaos ensues, they constantly spawn from 8 different locations.

In total, we have 6 colors, 3 health indicators, a score indicator, our inventory, and 3 potions.

In this game, where we try to manage color combinations and complete tasks, we also try many combos to escape.

# The system's color functions are as follows:

The primary colors are red, blue, and yellow-->These can only be collected

Secondary colors orange green purple-->These colors can be used in tasks involving both gathering and merging

The tertiary colors are brown, burgundy, emerald green, indigo, and mustard-->These 5 colors can only be used for task combinations and cannot be accumulated in the inventory

# Color combinations
Red x Yellow-->Orange

Red x Blue-->Purple

Blue x Yellow-->Green

Red x Green-->Brown

Red x Purple-->Burgundy

Blue x Green-->Emerald

Blue x Purple-->Indigo

Yellow x Yellow-->Mustard

Tasks only involve collecting and combining; for example, red can only be collected, but green can be collected and combined. Additionally, if a task involves collecting green, collecting yellow won't reduce your health because it's a related color.Chaos mode is activated if you use 1 out of every 3 potions.

1.Potion(ELIXIR OF BALANCE)-->If we choose the wrong color or an unrelated color, we won't lose a life for 5 seconds.

2.Potion(MAGNETIC ELIXIR)-->Whatever our current task is, those colors come towards us, making it much easier to complete the tasks.

3.Potion(QUEST POTION)-->Sadece mevcut hedefle ilişkili renkler ortaya çıkacak, bu da oyuncuya daha sorunsuz bir geçiş sağlayacak.
